Teacher portal

The teacher portal gives you full control: create, edit, publish and close assignments, grade submissions and manage accounts.

  • Create assignments with attachments and deadlines
  • Target a class/section or the whole college
  • See who submitted, who is late, who is missing
  • Grade with feedback and export marks to CSV
